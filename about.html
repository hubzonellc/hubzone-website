About Hub Zone

The hub where companies and people meet

Built on 30 years in Puerto Rico's staffing market, Hub Zone, LLC is now expanding to North and South Carolina.

Meet the founder

Pedro E. Rivera

Founder, Hub Zone, LLC

Pedro E. Rivera is the founder of Hub Zone, LLC. After 30 years in Puerto Rico's staffing market, he is expanding Hub Zone's services to the North and South Carolina job market.

Pedro specializes in four areas: finance, accounting, information technology and human resources. He works with both sides of the hire, helping companies find the right talent and helping candidates connect with companies and land the job.
